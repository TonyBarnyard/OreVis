## What a broker pays (monthly fee + 5% of commission)

- Solo Broker: $499/mo + 5% = 7.2% of expected commission
- Broker Team: $1,499/mo + 5% = 6.9% of expected commission
- Brokerage Firm: $3,999/mo + 5% = 7.0% of expected commission

Success-fee revenue assumes 75% of broker commission is reported and collected, starting 3 months after a broker joins. The full and lean team plans are still in the workbook (team_plan row in Assumptions).
