## Value to the broker (does each plan pay for itself?)

- Solo Broker $999/mo: returns 7.4x its price (4.4% of the broker's commission)
- Broker Team $2,499/mo: returns 8.3x its price (3.1% of commission)
- Brokerage Firm $6,999/mo: returns 8.7x its price (3.5% of commission)

Value = hours saved (6 per deal at $75/hr) + commission on the extra loans the broker can close with that time (0.25 / 0.5 / 1 extra loan a month). The target is at least 5x. See the Pricing sheet.
